## 💡 Key Features at a Glance

### Export Students
- **Formats:** CSV, Excel, JSON, PDF
- **Grade Selection:** 7, 8, 9, 10 or all
- **Custom Fields:** Choose what to include
- **Advanced Options:** Available for power users

### Archive & Transition
- **Automatic Promotion:** Grade to next level
- **Graduation Handling:** Remove Grade 10
- **Data Preservation:** Records backed up forever
- **Audit Trail:** Logged for compliance
- **Safe Execution:** Transaction-based (all or nothing)

### Archive Management
- **History View:** See all past archives
- **Grade Distribution:** Breakdown per grade
- **Status Tracking:** Pending/Completed
- **Detailed Reports:** Full archive details
- **Timestamped:** Know when each happened

### In 4 Steps:

1. **Run Database Setup**
   ```sql
   Run: install_archive_tables.sql
   ```

2. **Verify Tables**
   ```sql
   SHOW TABLES LIKE 'student_archive%';
   ```

3. **Access Feature**
   ```
   Login → Click "Student Archive" in sidebar
   ```

4. **Start Using**
   ```
   Export or archive students
   ```

**Total Time:** 5 minutes
